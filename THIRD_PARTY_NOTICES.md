## System libraries

The Nix package links the libraries the app and the CLI need from nixpkgs: WebKit, GTK3, GTK4, `gtk4-layer-shell`, Graphene, PipeWire, Pango, Cairo, GdkPixbuf, Wayland, libxkbcommon and Tesseract with its model. Their licenses remain the upstream projects' licenses; the package is a redistribution, so the text of each license family is in [LICENSES/](LICENSES/) alongside the crate list in [docs/legal/dependencies.md](docs/legal/dependencies.md).
